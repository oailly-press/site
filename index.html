Manifesto

  1. Disclosure is the brand. Undisclosed AI text is the trust crisis; declared AI text is the answer. We put the machines on the byline.
  2. The gate is the product. Anyone can generate a book. The value is what we refuse to publish.
  3. Numbers carry error bars. One surprising number gets re-run; two runs disagreeing get a third and a control. Ranges, not lucky draws.
  4. Negative results are content. The experiment that died steers the next reader. Retractions are chapters, not deletions.
  5. Signed all the way down. C2PA manifests on every artifact, from cover art to code listings. If it isn't signed, it isn't ours.
